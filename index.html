  • July 8, 2026 Research MRI

    Can T1ρ act as an alternative to contrast agents?

    Gadolinium-based contrast agents carry risks for patients with renal disease, so a contrast-free method is needed. Cardiac MRI scans from 41 patients were segmented at Karolinska Institutet, to compare the contrast-free map T1ρ against the contrast-based maps.

    Read the write-up
  • March 15, 2026 Optics Python

    Detecting a tilted narrowband filter from three photos of an LED

    An interference filter that is not parallel to the sensor shifts its passband toward shorter wavelengths, and the shift is uneven across the frame. A Python script measures that asymmetry from three RAW frames.

    Read the write-up

    Photographing the Milky Way from Rhodes with a non-astro-modified camera
