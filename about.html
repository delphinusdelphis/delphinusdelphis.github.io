About

Ana Kostic

High school student in Sweden, studying natural sciences.

I study natural sciences at a high school in Sweden. I am passionate about physics, and it is the subject I intend to continue with.

In the summer of 2026 I carried out a project at Karolinska Institutet, Department of Molecular Medicine and Surgery, through Rays – Research Academy for Young Scientists. The project compared a contrast-free cardiac MRI map, T1ρ, against the contrast-based maps used in the clinic. Gadolinium-based contrast agents carry risks for patients with renal disease, so a contrast-free alternative is needed. The project was supervised by PhD student Pontus Pandurevic.

Outside school I do deep-sky and wide-field astrophotography. This involves interference filter coatings, sensor dynamic-range curves and filter alignment, which is where the other two projects on this site come from.
